# Delivery roadmap

Each phase is a tested vertical slice.

1. **Foundation (delivered):** tenancy, auth, RBAC primitives, migrations, operational shell, real empty dashboard.
2. **Organization administration (delivered):** organization settings, branches, departments, user lifecycle, teams, territories, 15 configurable role templates, permission management, and audited mutations.
3. **Lead-to-customer (delivered):** sources, assignment, duplicate protection, qualification state machine, scoring, activities, follow-ups, notes, timeline, lost reasons, CSV import architecture, conversion, pipeline views, ageing analysis, and permission-aware Customer 360.
4. **Projects and inventory (delivered):** audited project, tower, floor, and unit CRUD; configurable areas, facing, pricing, amenities and metadata; tenant-scoped availability search; approval-aware soft/hard holds with history and automatic expiry; and transaction-locked booking/status transitions with database uniqueness protection.
5. **Sales execution (delivered):** scheduling, salesperson assignment, interested-unit shortlists, check-in/check-out, feedback, calendar, versioned price lists, server-calculated cost sheets, enforced discount approval matrices, quotation history, and PDF generation architecture.
6. **Booking and KYC (delivered):** private customer/booking-linked document requests, secure
   uploads, reviewer assignment, expiry, verified/rejected KYC decisions, version history and
   authenticated downloads; transaction-locked booking creation from quotation and hold,
   applicants, payment plans, broker/salesperson ownership, financing checks, payment verification,
   ordered approvals, confirmation, rejection/cancellation release, and audit trails. Transfers and
   agreements remain planned.
7. **Revenue and collections (delivered):** payment plans and ageing, server-derived demands,
   partial payments, reconciliation records, installment allocation, receipts, customer ledger,
   outstanding/overdue portfolios, calculated penalties/interest, waivers, and approval-controlled
   refunds with allocation and ledger reversal.
8. **Partners and financing:** lead protection, commissions/payouts, loan workflow.
9. **Possession and service:** construction updates, possession, handover, service requests.
10. **Rental operations:** tenants, leases, invoices, rent collection, maintenance.
11. **Automation and intelligence:** notifications, workflows, approvals, reports and dashboards.
