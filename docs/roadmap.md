# Delivery roadmap

Each phase is a tested vertical slice.

1. **Foundation (current):** tenancy, auth, RBAC primitives, migrations, operational shell, real empty dashboard.
2. **Organization administration:** branches, departments, user lifecycle, 15 configurable role templates, permission management, audit viewer.
3. **Lead-to-customer:** sources, assignment, qualification state machine, activities, conversion, Customer 360.
4. **Projects and inventory:** projects, towers, floors, units, pricing, availability and locked transitions.
5. **Sales execution:** visits, quotations, cost sheets, discount approval, expiring unit holds.
6. **Booking and KYC:** documents, transactional booking, approval, cancellation, transfer, agreements.
7. **Revenue and collections:** plans, installments, demands, payments, receipts, ledger, overdue/refunds.
8. **Partners and financing:** lead protection, commissions/payouts, loan workflow.
9. **Possession and service:** construction updates, possession, handover, service requests.
10. **Rental operations:** tenants, leases, invoices, rent collection, maintenance.
11. **Automation and intelligence:** notifications, workflows, approvals, reports and dashboards.
